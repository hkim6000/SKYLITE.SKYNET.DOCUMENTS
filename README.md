Overview:
ServiceNet is a web-based field service management platform for companies that schedule technicians, 
perform on-site work, and track the parts and outcomes of every job. 
It brings the whole operation together: customer accounts and locations, 
a scheduling calendar with technician availability, dispatch and progress tracking, 
quality assurance sign-off, parts inventory, a service-and-parts catalog, and reporting. 
Customers can request service themselves through a public portal, 
and staff are kept in the loop through in-app and email notifications at each stage.
